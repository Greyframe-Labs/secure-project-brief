This utility encrypts the selected MP4 entirely in your browser. The source video and passphrase are not uploaded by this page.

Use a unique passphrase of at least 16 characters and deliver it separately from the page URL.

Output format: SPB1 + 16-byte salt + 12-byte IV + AES-256-GCM ciphertext. Key derivation uses PBKDF2/SHA-256 with 600,000 iterations. The finished file should be placed at assets/recruiter-brief.enc.
